# PLANNING ENGINE

Planning components include MedicalSafetyPolicyEngine, SafetyEligibilityFilter, ExerciseCandidateBuilder semantics, DailyPlanningEngine, WeeklyPlanningEngine, MonthlyProgressionEngine, duration fitting, variety rotation, conservative modifications, and explanation generation. Progression is blocked after pain increases, repeated low readiness, symptoms, incomplete sessions, safety blocks, or clinician restrictions.
