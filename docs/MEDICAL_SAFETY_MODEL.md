# MEDICAL SAFETY MODEL

MoveInRange is not an emergency service. If severe symptoms occur, stop exercising and contact local emergency services. The policy engine runs before plans, workout start/resume, progression, diabetes insights, coaching, and notifications. Outcomes are READY, READY_WITH_MODIFICATIONS, LOW_INTENSITY_ONLY, DELAY_AND_RECHECK, FOLLOW_CLINICIAN_PLAN, and BLOCK_AND_SHOW_SAFETY_MESSAGE. All thresholds are draft until clinical review.
